How to play

Open every square that doesn't hide a mine. A number shows how many mines touch that square, including diagonals. Your first dig is always safe.

Touch

  • Tap to dig
  • Long-press to flag
  • Use the Dig / Flag switch to swap them

Mouse

  • Left click to dig
  • Right click to flag
  • Click a number whose flags are all placed to open its neighbours

Keyboard

  • ←↑→↓ move
  • Enter / Space dig
  • F flag · M switch mode
  • N new game · ? help
